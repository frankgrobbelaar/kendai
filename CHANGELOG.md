# Changelog

What changed in each version of kendai, newest first, in plain words. A release moves what's under
"Unreleased" under its version; see [docs/releasing.md](docs/releasing.md).

## Unreleased

- Kendai's first shape: the Library, Jobs and Settings, in Washi, light or dark, with a bar along
  the bottom on phones and a rail down the side on tablets, and an e-ink mode in black and white
  that turns itself on on e-ink readers.
- The library on the phone. The first run asks for All files access in plain words, suggests the
  folders that hold books, and finds them, with their titles, authors, series and covers. Home shows
  what was added recently; Folders shows the real folders that hold books, as a list or as covers,
  sorted by title, author or date added. Kendai looks again each time it opens and when the list is
  pulled down, and the library's folders can be changed in Settings without touching a file.
